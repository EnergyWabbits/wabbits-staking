# 🎟️ Scratch Tickets

Scratch Tickets are the primary entry point into the Energy Wabbits Lunaverse ecosystem.

Designed to be simple, accessible, and rewarding, Scratch Tickets allow participants to instantly engage with the platform and compete for a variety of rewards.

No NFT ownership is required to participate.

---

## Ticket Price

- $1 Per Ticket

Anyone can purchase and play Scratch Tickets.

---

## How It Works

Each Scratch Ticket contains a 3-match game.

Players reveal symbols hidden on the ticket.

### Winning Ticket

Match 3 identical symbols and win the prize associated with that symbol.

Winning tickets may unlock a variety of rewards depending on the active reward pool.

### Non-Winning Ticket

If a winning combination is not revealed, players receive an LDZ consolation reward.

This ensures every Scratch Ticket provides value back to the participant.

---

## Possible Rewards

Winning Scratch Tickets may award:

- LDZ Rewards
- Wheel Spins
- Memecoins (Partner Tokens)
- Sol Rewards

Available rewards may change throughout the life of the platform.

---

## LDZ Consolation Rewards

Participants who do not reveal a winning combination will receive an LDZ consolation reward.

LDZ consolation rewards are designed to ensure every ticket contributes to ecosystem participation and engagement.

Consolation reward amounts may vary.

---

## Wheel Spin Rewards

Some winning Scratch Tickets may award Wheel Spins.

Wheel Spins provide access to the premium reward layer of the ecosystem and offer opportunities for larger rewards.

Winning a Wheel Spin through a Scratch Ticket allows participants to continue progressing through the Energy Wabbits Lunaverse reward system.

---

## Prize Pool Allocation

A portion of Scratch Ticket revenue is allocated toward reward funding.

Revenue generated through Scratch Tickets may support:

- Reward Pools
- NFT Staking Rewards
- Platform Development
- Marketing Initiatives
- Treasury Reserves
- Future Ecosystem Expansion

Allocation structures may evolve over time to ensure long-term sustainability.

---

## Sustainability

The Scratch Ticket system is designed to balance rewarding gameplay with ecosystem growth.

Reward structures, prize pools, and available prizes may be adjusted over time as the platform evolves.

The objective is to create a sustainable experience that benefits both participants and the broader Energy Wabbits Lunaverse ecosystem.
